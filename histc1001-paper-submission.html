📚 Chicago-Style Footnotes: How to Add Them Here

Type each footnote inside double square brackets, immediately after the punctuation that ends the sentence or clause. When you download your paper, each note becomes a superscript number with the note printed at the bottom of the same page. Put titles of books, newspapers, and magazines in *asterisks* so they print in italics.

What you type
The Sedition Act made it a crime to publish "false, scandalous and malicious writing" against the government.[[Sedition Act, July 14, 1798, sec. 2.]]

Your paper will show a small number after "government." and the note at the bottom of the page. Do not type your own numbers; they are added for you.

First note for a magazine article (The Atlantic)
[[Jeffrey Rosen, "The Nightmare of Despotism," *The Atlantic*, [date], [page or URL].]]

Use the title and date from the copy you read. If the online title differs from the print title, cite the one you read.

Primary document
[[Sedition Act, July 14, 1798, sec. 2.]]
[[Thomas Jefferson, Kentucky Resolutions, 1798, [where you read it].]]

Give the author (if any), the document's title, its date, and where you found it (packet page, URL, or archive).

Podcast episode
[[*Civics 101*, "Democratic Norms," episode 105, [date], [host or network].]]
Your interview
[[Interview with [Name or "anonymous interviewee"], by the author, [place or phone/video], [date].]]

Use the name option your interviewee agreed to.

Later notes for the same source (short form)
[[Rosen, "Nightmare of Despotism."]]

After the first full note, use the author's last name and a shortened title. Use a page number when the source has one.

💡 Tips
  • The bracket examples above show the form of each note. Replace the [brackets] with the real details from the source you used.
  • Every source in a footnote should appear in your bibliography, and the reverse.
  • Say whose definition of the norms you are using, and cite it.
  • Every note must be closed with ]]. The page will tell you if one is open or empty.

📑 Bibliography Format (Chicago Notes-Bibliography)

List every source you cited, one entry per line, in alphabetical order by the author's last name (or by title when there is no author). Your downloaded paper adds the hanging indent for you. Put titles of books, newspapers, and magazines in *asterisks* for italics.
