HIST C1001 · Reading & Analysis Labs

Short, interactive extra-credit activities that follow the last four weeks of the course. Read the assigned chapter first, then complete the lab.

How it works. Each lab has sorting/ordering/matching activities, a short source analysis, a six-question reading check, and a short written response. Auto-checked items give instant feedback; your written work is reviewed by your instructor. Submit once per lab, then download the PDF of your work. Save it for your files and upload it to the matching Canvas assignment.
